Saffron Line is a complete cooking game in a warm ember kitchen. You are the
chef on a 180-second shift: take ingredients from the pantry, chop them on the
board, simmer them on the stove or grill them on the grate, plate the dish,
and ring the bell at the serve window before the ticket's bar runs out.
12 recipes combine 6 ingredients in different ways across 6 stations, and
3 service rushes make tickets arrive faster and expire sooner as the shift
heats up. Score for every step done right, combos for clean serves in a row,
and stars at the end of the shift.

Three systems run under the counter. **Doneness:** anything on the stove
or grill cooks through a colour-zoned bar — UNDER, then the green PERFECT
window, then BURNT. Click the station to take it off in the green for
+50; leave it too long and it burns away, and a burnt item on the plate
costs −25. **Alternate routes:** 7 of the 12 recipes accept a second
route (grill the tomato first for a Smokey Tomato Soup, pan-toast the
Cheese Sandwich) worth +40 a serve — the ticket prints both routes.
**VIP tickets:** gold orders worth 3× the score on a shorter clock.

Controls: move with WASD / arrow keys, take an ingredient with keys 1–6 or
by clicking the pantry, interact with E / Space, pause with P, restart with
R, mute with M. Everything is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized —
no external assets, no network at runtime.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
